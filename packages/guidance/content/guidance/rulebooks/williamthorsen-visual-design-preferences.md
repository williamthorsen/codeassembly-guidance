# William Thorsen's visual-design preferences

Taste that applies on top of {rulebook:visual-design-conventions}, which states the principles that hold whatever the taste. A preference here never relaxes one of those principles.

## Colour scheme

Prefer a quiet colour scheme to a monochrome one: muted hues on neutral surfaces, each hue encoding a kind or a state. A monochrome scheme leaves weight and style to do all the encoding, which the colour-as-encoding rule of {rulebook:visual-design-conventions} rules out, and a loud scheme spends the reader's attention on decoration.
